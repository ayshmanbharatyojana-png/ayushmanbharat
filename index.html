About PM-JAY

Scheme design
₹5 lakh health cover

Annual health cover per eligible family for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation, subject to scheme rules.

Cashless treatment

Eligible beneficiaries can access covered treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals.

Nationwide portability

Eligible beneficiaries can access covered services at empanelled hospitals across India.
